# Reika — Pose and Turnaround Specification v1.0

## Canonical views
1. Front: hands gently joined, upright balanced pose, all nine tails visibly fanned around silhouette.
2. Three-quarter left: slight torso rotation, same face and costume; tails remain countable.
3. Side left: accurate head/ear profile and dress silhouette; distinguish the tail roots rather than inventing new tails.
4. Back: show attachment point and ordering of nine tails plus hair and gown ornament structure.
5. Three-quarter right: mirror body orientation while preserving asymmetrical details from the original artwork.

## Interaction poses
- Greeting bow (subtle, noble).
- Listening with head tilt.
- Thinking with one hand near chin.
- Explaining with open palm.
- Presenting a document or tablet.
- Relaxed seated posture.
- Protective stance, magic tails spread.
- Farewell wave.

## Art production acceptance tests
- Consistent adult anatomy, height ratio and facial landmarks.
- Two fox ears, no human ears prominent beneath them.
- Exactly nine tails; overlapping tails documented in back view.
- Constant costume layers, gold trim and hair ornament topology.
- Model sheet: plain background, fixed neutral lighting, front/back/side and 3/4 views at matched scale.
- Rig preparation: avoid cutting tails or hands off at frame boundary.
