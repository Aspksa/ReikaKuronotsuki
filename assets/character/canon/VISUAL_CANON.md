# Reika Kuronotsuki — Official Visual Canon v1.0

**Name:** Reika Kuronotsuki (黒ノ月 麗華)  
**Age:** 37 (adult)  
**Species:** Nine-tailed kitsune  
**Role:** Personal AI assistant and noble fox spirit

## Immutable character features

- Exactly nine separate large fluffy fox tails, silver-white with pearl highlights and cool shadows.
- Two white fox ears with pale pink inner fur; never cat ears.
- Bright sky-blue almond eyes, expressive, with soft highlights.
- Very long flowing silver-white/pearl hair, gently waved; warm highlights permitted from lanterns.
- Fair porcelain complexion, graceful adult proportions, approximately 172 cm without ears.
- Calm, intelligent, kind, confident and noble expression.

## Official costume: Imperial Moon

Luxurious eastern-fantasy ceremonial attire mixing kimono and hanfu details: white silk, black bodice/collar/panels, refined gold flower embroidery, flowing sleeves, layered long skirt, black-and-gold waist decoration, gold floral hair pins and dangling tassels, refined matching footwear.

## Environment and rendering

The signature portrait places Reika in an ornate moonlit eastern palace with golden lanterns, cherry blossoms, large windows, full moon, reflective polished floor, delicate petals and both warm lantern light and cool moonlight. Premium detailed anime-fantasy illustration; realistic silk, fur and metal texture with controlled highlights.

## Animation and expression

- **Idle:** gentle breathing, blinking, subtle ear motions and independently swaying tails.
- **Happy:** warm smile, attentive ears, playful light tail movements.
- **Focused:** serious eyes, controlled upright ears and slightly lifted tails.
- **Protective:** assertive posture, wide spread tail fan.
- **Magic:** subtle blue eye light and silver-blue accents in the raised tails.

## Consistency rules

Preserve Reika's adult face, nine tails, blue eyes, silver-white fur, white fox ears, ornamental gold details and noble style in every render, portrait, Live2D rig or 3D model. Do not arbitrarily change the silhouette or proportions. Image generation may miscount overlapping tails: review manually before accepting new art.
