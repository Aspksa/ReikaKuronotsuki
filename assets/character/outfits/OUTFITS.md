# Reika — Outfit Library v1.0

All costumes use the exact same Reika identity: blue eyes, long pearl-silver hair, white fox ears and nine silver-white tails. Costumes are optional states; **Imperial Moon remains the default**.

## Design control
Change costume only when explicitly selected; do not change face, age, ear shape, eye color, hair base color, or tail count. Maintain natural movement clearance for nine large tails. Each outfit needs full-body front/back and detail closeups before production approval.
