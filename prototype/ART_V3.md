# Detailed SVG art v3

This additive art layer provides moonlit palace geometry, nine sets of silver fur highlights, gold embroidery, hair ornaments and atmospheric lighting accents. Individual tail decorations follow their parent tail transforms. The v2 rig and controls remain unchanged.

This is a stylized vector study rather than a full fidelity Live2D model. Run the prototype locally in a browser to verify appearance and accessibility. Full portrait fidelity requires separately designed layered artwork.
